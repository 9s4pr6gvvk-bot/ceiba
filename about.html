About Gerry Spencer

Rooted in experience.
Focused on what's possible.

Gerry Spencer — Life & Transformational Coach · Founder of The CEIBA Method™

The Professional

Over two decades of experience — working with individuals, organisations and everything life brings in between.

ICF Qualified Coach NLP Master Practitioner Management & Leadership Business Owner Wellbeing in the Workplace

I have spent over two decades working with people navigating change — individuals finding their way through significant life transitions, and organisations supporting their people through uncertainty and growth. As a management trainer, a leadership coach, a business owner and a mother, I have worked across every context where human beings need to find their ground again.

Across all of it, one belief has remained constant: people are more capable than they realise, and real growth always begins from the inside out.

I bring both professional experience and genuine personal insight to every coaching relationship. What drives me is simple — helping people reconnect with who they are, find their direction, and move forward with real confidence and clarity.

Where CEIBA Began

A road in Costa Rica. An ancient tree.
A moment that stayed with me.

I was travelling solo around Costa Rica — the first time I had done anything like that since my younger years. I arrived mentally exhausted and in the middle of a significant period of change in my own life. I was already working on a new business but hadn't yet found the heart of what I was building.

One morning I rode my bike down an old road and into the dense forest, and I came across a tree that took my breath away. The roots were enormous — spreading across the ground in every direction, heavy and ancient. The trunk was vast. And when I looked up, the canopy was catching the light high above me with the leaves shining right at the top. It was a real moment for me.

“I mentioned to a Costa Rican woman nearby that it was the most amazing tree I had ever seen. She said its name: CEIBA.”

Returning home, it kept coming to me. What struck me was something simple — that everything visible above the ground is a direct reflection of what exists beneath it. The roots go as deep as the tree grows tall. You cannot separate the two.

That felt true about people too. And it became the foundation of everything CEIBA is.

What I believe

The creativity, the movement, the possibility we want to reach for — all of it depends on the foundations we have built inside ourselves. The self-knowledge. The grounding. The clarity about who we actually are and what genuinely matters. When change shakes those foundations, everything becomes harder. When we rebuild them, everything becomes possible again. That is the work CEIBA does.

Outside of coaching you will find me outdoors — especially by the sea. Looking for new places and adventure, playing tennis and finding humour in all of those places with family and friends.

I have always loved trees and the quiet majesty of them. The CEIBA was not a coincidence. It was a recognition.
