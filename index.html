About CEIBA

Rooted in experience.
Focused on what's possible.

For much of my career, I have worked with individuals, helping them navigate change and uncertainty, as well as with organisations, supporting people, motivating teams and helping them reach their potential. Then I experienced a significant change in my own life, and I discovered that real growth is nothing like a decision. It asks something far more demanding, and far more rewarding.

The resources, the resilience, the strength you need are already inside you. They haven’t gone — they’ve simply become hard to access. CEIBA is a way to find your roots, reconnect with who you truly are, and from there, discover what you’re capable of.

01Reconnect

Roots

Who am I, and what do I want to reconnect with?

Before we move forward, it helps to understand where we’re standing. Together we’ll explore your story, identity, values, strengths and wellbeing — and the patterns and beliefs shaping how you see yourself.

We’ll look at what became harder to access during change, and begin reconnecting with the resources already within you.

IdentityValuesWellbeing
02Strengthen

Trunk

What do I need to feel strong enough to move forward?

With greater self-awareness comes the chance to build from a stronger foundation. We’ll explore confidence, self-trust and resilience — the internal and external resources that carry you through uncertainty.

We’ll build the capacity to stand more confidently in who you are, and the strength to move even when the way isn’t yet clear.

ConfidenceResilienceSelf-trust
03Explore

Branches

What could be possible if I allowed myself to explore?

Once we feel more grounded, our perspective widens. Together we’ll explore new ways of thinking, challenge assumptions and make space for curiosity, creativity and possibility.

We’ll consider what you’d do differently, what you’d explore if you trusted yourself more, and what lies beyond the choices you can currently see.

CuriosityCreativityPossibility
04Grow

Leaves

What am I ready to do, create or become?

Growth becomes meaningful when it shows up in our lives. Together we’ll turn insight into movement — the choices you want to make, the changes you want to create, and the practical steps toward what matters.

We’ll experiment, learn from what happens, and build the confidence to keep moving forward well beyond our work together.

ActionMovementTransformation

Work With Me

This is where it starts.

If anything on this page has resonated — if you recognise yourself in the people I work with — the first step is simply a conversation. No commitment, no pressure. Just an honest discussion about where you are and whether CEIBA is the right fit for you.

A clear path. Entirely yours.

The CEIBA Method follows a clear path — Roots, Trunk, Branches, Leaves — but how we walk it adapts entirely to you. Your story, your pace, your goals.

  • A free 45-minute discovery conversation
  • A 90-minute deep dive session — your story, your starting point, your framework
  • 6–8 fortnightly coaching sessions through the four CEIBA stages
  • Personalised resources and reflective work between every session
  • Message support between sessions — you're never left stuck alone
  • A foundation document you keep and return to long after we finish
